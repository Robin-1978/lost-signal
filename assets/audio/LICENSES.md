# Audio asset license registry

The WAV files listed in `manifest.json` are project-authored presentation
assets. They were generated from the deterministic source recipe recorded in
the manifest and are distributed under `LicenseRef-LostSignal-Project`.
They contain no third-party recordings or field recordings.

The mechanical buses are evidence-driven at playback time: the files are only
sound identities, while gain and pitch come from completed SIM frame evidence.
The environment bed is a low-level neutral ventilation/noise bed. It is not a
sensor, a score input, or a source of ROBOT observations.
